We are currently inviting applications for our **Short-Term Visiting Scientists Program**. This initiative offers up to four awards to support one-month visits from external, established researchers and professors.

Each selected fellow will receive approximately **€2,500 per month** to help cover travel and living expenses. The primary aim of this program is to foster high-impact collaborations between Université Côte d'Azur (UniCA) and external research groups, while providing a unique opportunity for leading experts to engage with the LIGHTFAB network and its state-of-the-art facilities.

### Eligibility
* **Target Profile:** This call is exclusively open to external, established researchers and professors. *(Note: PhD students and postdocs are not eligible for this specific call; see below).*
* **Collaborative Focus:** The proposed visit must demonstrate a clear intent to collaborate with UniCA research groups and actively engage with the LIGHTFAB network.

### Important Dates
* **Call opening:** Early September 2026
* **Announcement of selection decisions:** Early November 2026

### How to Submit
To facilitate the review process, please submit your application as a **single PDF document** (2 pages maximum, excluding CV).

Your PDF should clearly outline:
* **The Visit Objective:** A description of the scientific activities, seminars, or research you plan to conduct during your stay.
* **The Collaborative Impact:** An explanation of how your visit will foster synergy between your home institution and UniCA/LIGHTFAB.
* **Brief CV:** A short academic biography highlighting your current position and key expertise.

Submit your application via email to: [lightfab-2027@univ-cotedazur.fr](mailto:lightfab-2027@univ-cotedazur.fr)
